## Project-writing decisions (before any render)

- Shot 32 in the screenplay is a rapid comic sequence with cuts inside it (splat -> Zlata resisting -> glare ->
  laugh -> room erupts -> final wide) — one 2-4 s take cannot hold it. Split into shots 32-38 (seven consecutive
  shots, ~21 s). The reel runs ~115 s instead of 92; the kickoff allows this ("give them the time they need").
- The screenplay's narration does not physically fit shot 5 (two long narrator sentences, 12.7-16.5, then the
  Tsarina at 17.0). Gave shot 6 dur 4.0 (was 2.2) and placed narrator "She was starting to regret it." at 17.2
  and "Please, darling." at 19.5. Other speaking shots got small dur increases (12, 19, 24-27) so no line
  overruns its shot; every line is word-for-word. Narrator starts for lines after shot 6 sit on their screenplay
  shot in the shifted timeline.
- The witch's line splits at the screenplay's own "A beat." into two narration entries (54.0, 55.8).
- Palace maids and the two door servants are extras: described in each still, never `[[cast]]` (first-test
  lesson: one cast reference per extra duplicates faces). The servant's announcement line carries an inline
  `voice`, not a `who`.
- Shot 1 is text-to-image (no set, no cast) and its words are `[[overlay]]`s; the still says "no text, no
  letters". The ice sculpture (shot 14) is also cast=[] and fully described in the still, so the edit cannot put
  the real Zlata inside the cloth reveal.
- Speaking shots: `motion` names the speaker and closes everyone else's mouth; take audio muted on them so the
  voice track is the only speech. Diegetic sound kept/ducked on hoof, cane tok, cloth, cart, splat and laughter
  shots.
- Voice: narrator = dry British RP deadpan (screenplay); zlata/tsarina/witch/narsissus voices from the CAST
  section. Music prompt from the MUSIC section, 116 s to cover the whole reel.
